# Arcane Friction

An industrial fantasy expert-style modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1.

Arcane Friction focuses on:
- progression
- automation
- exploration
- RPG combat
- industrial infrastructure
- magic integration
- handcrafted progression systems

This is not a kitchen sink modpack.

Every major system is interconnected through:
- custom recipes
- progression gating
- exploration requirements
- scaling difficulty
- cross-mod integrations

---

# Main Features

## Industrial Progression

Progress through multiple technological ages:

Create → Immersive Engineering → Mekanism → AE2 → Draconic Evolution

Build:
- factories
- rail infrastructure
- power grids
- digital automation systems
- large-scale industrial networks

---

## Arcane Systems

Magic is fully integrated into progression.

Featuring:
- Ars Nouveau
- Occultism
- Hexerei
- Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks

Technology and magic depend on each other through custom crafting and progression systems.

---

## Exploration Matters

The world contains:
- dangerous structures
- unique dimensions
- dungeon progression
- exploration-gated resources
- rare crafting materials

Exploration is required for progression.

---

## Dynamic Difficulty

The world evolves as players advance.

Progression unlocks:
- stronger enemies
- elite mobs
- new world dangers
- advanced encounters
- escalating combat challenges

---

# Philosophy

Arcane Friction is built around one core principle:

Progress should feel earned.

Players evolve from primitive survival into architects of industrial-magical civilizations.

---

# Recommended Specs

Recommended:
- 10-12 GB RAM allocated
- SSD storage
- modern CPU

Chunk pregeneration is highly recommended for servers.
